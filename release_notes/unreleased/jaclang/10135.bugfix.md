- **Fix: build-kit seals the native kernel again, and the checker gaps behind the failure are closed**: a dict literal keyed by enum members and indexed by a `str` stopped the kernel seal with E1053. Container literals with no expected type now widen enum members to their enum class. A subscript index that no `__getitem__` signature accepts is reported at the index, instead of becoming a silent `Unknown` that failed at the next use. `k in container` keeps the members of `k` that can compare equal to the container's key type, so a guarded lookup is accepted. An `is None` check on a type that cannot hold None narrows that arm to `Never` instead of `NoneType`, so the arm no longer reports errors against `NoneType` or leaks it past the `if`. Attribute access, calls and comparisons on `Never` report nothing.
